Runs in your browser · No game injection, memory reading, or automatic gameplay. Screen-sharing permission is requested each session; EXP uses pixel templates with conservative OCR fallback, and mesos use a separate OCR worker. OCR uses Tesseract.js. Uncertain OCR values are withheld; check the diagnostics for rejected readings. EXP requirements are bundled locally and do not use a live third-party service. Potion tracking is disabled pending redesign.
