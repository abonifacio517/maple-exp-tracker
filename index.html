Runs in your browser · No game injection, memory reading, or automatic gameplay. Screen-sharing permission is requested each session; EXP uses pixel templates with conservative OCR fallback, and mesos use a separate OCR worker. OCR uses Tesseract.js. Uncertain mesos OCR values are displayed as unverified guesses; only repeated higher-confidence or manual readings affect mesos/hour. EXP requirements are bundled locally and do not use a live third-party service. Consumable counts are entered manually; published NPC shop prices provide estimated costs without double-counting the displayed mesos balance.
